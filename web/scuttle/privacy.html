Scuttle Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Applies to Scuttle: Boat Sandbox for iPhone and iPad

Scuttle does not collect personal information. There are no accounts of our own, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers run by the developer. The only things that ever leave your device are what you send through Apple's Game Center or to nearby players when you choose to play multiplayer, both described below.

What stays on your device

Scuttle saves a few things on your device using standard iOS app preferences: your settings (things like gravity, sound volume, and render scale), your personal best Arcade scores, and, for nearby play, a random identifier and a "Sailor" callsign that the app makes up for itself. None of this is sent to the developer, and all of it is deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center (optional)

Online co-op, Versus, leaderboards, and challenges use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple handles your sign-in, matchmaking, invites, and the match traffic between players. When you finish an Arcade run, your score and level are posted to Scuttle's Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them alongside your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out or change what other players see in the Game Center section of the Settings app. Solo play and nearby multiplayer work without Game Center.

Nearby play (optional)

Nearby co-op and Versus work without the internet, over your local Wi-Fi network or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. The first time you open the nearby screen, iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. While you host a game, your device announces it to nearby devices with your Game Center nickname (or your Sailor callsign if you are not signed in), the names of the players aboard, and the game's progress. Gameplay is then sent directly between the devices in the game. None of it goes through a server. You can turn off local network access for Scuttle at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Tracking

Scuttle does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier. It includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

Scuttle is rated 9+ for infrequent cartoon or fantasy violence and guns or other weapons. It collects no information from anyone, including children. Parents can limit Game Center features, such as multiplayer with others and friend requests, with Screen Time content and privacy restrictions.

Purchases

Scuttle is a one-time purchase on the App Store. Apple processes the transaction; the app itself never sees your payment information. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
