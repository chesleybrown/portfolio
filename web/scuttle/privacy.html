Scuttle Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · Applies to Scuttle: Boat Sandbox for iOS

Scuttle does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Full stop.

What the app does with data

Scuttle has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that talk to the internet. The only data it writes is your in-app settings (things like gravity, sound volume, and render scale), which are stored on your device using standard iOS app preferences. That data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Tracking

Scuttle does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Scuttle is rated 9+ for mild cartoon violence. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Purchases

Scuttle is a one-time purchase on the App Store. Apple processes the transaction; the app itself never sees your payment information. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: see the Scuttle support page.
