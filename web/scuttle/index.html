What is Scuttle?

Scuttle is a $1.99 boat sinking game for iPhone and iPad with 54 hulls, including the Titanic. Drag one into the sea, then hit it with anything from arrows to lava, a tsunami or a nuke. You get Sandbox and Arcade, co-op and Versus for 2-4 players, and no ads.

A sea full of ways to lose a boat

Sandbox and Arcade

54 hulls, one drawer

Dinghies and tugs, galleons, container ships, aircraft carriers, supertankers, an oil rig with a live well, and the Titanic. Drag one out of the boat drawer and drop it anywhere: in the sky, on the water, or under it. The sea keeps running while you browse.

Flood, burn, split, capsize

Every hull is cells. Wood splinters, steel buckles, and water rushes into whatever you open. Arrows, missiles, torpedoes, lasers, bombs, mines, TNT, asteroids, and a nuke. Weather sinks them too: storms, hurricanes and tornadoes.

Tsunamis and lava

Split the seafloor and a pressure front climbs to the surface, then a wave rolls out both ways and carries boats up and over its crest. Pour lava on a deck and watch it melt through.

A seabed worth wrecking

Kelp, coral, crabs and garden eels on the sand, with layered rock, fossils and buried treasure underneath. Bombs dig craters, lasers cut trenches, and silt clouds burst up when a wreck hits bottom.

Up to 4 players

Co-op and Versus for 2-4 players, over Game Center or nearby Wi-Fi. Everyone sees the same sea. 8 Game Center leaderboards.

No ads. No analytics.

One purchase. No accounts for solo play, no purchases inside the app, nothing tracking you. Online play is Apple's Game Center. Nearby play never leaves the room.

Build notes

  • Swift and SwiftUIThe whole app. Menus, sandbox, arcade, the lobby.
  • MetalThe water and the sprites are drawn on the GPU.
  • Box2DRigid bodies. Hulls made of cells that flood, burn and come apart.
  • LockstepEvery device runs the same simulation. Game Center online, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi nearby. No game server of mine, and nothing to phone home.

Support

Found a bug, or have an idea for the sandbox? Email me@chesleybrown.ca. Include your device model and iOS or iPadOS version if you are reporting a problem. The privacy policy is a page of its own.

Why is it running slowly?
In Scuttle, open Settings and lower the Particle budget, or set Render scale to 0.75x. Cell size 4 px is the fastest simulation.
Why don't nearby games show up?
Nearby play needs permission to find devices on your local network. Turn on Scuttle in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on every device, then open the nearby screen again.
Why won't online play start?
Online co-op, Versus and leaderboards need you to be signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Nearby play and everything else work without it.
Why does the app only work sideways?
That's intended. Scuttle runs sideways only, so the sea gets the full width of the screen.
How do I reset everything?
Settings › Restore Defaults puts every slider and toggle back to how it shipped.
Where are the third-party licenses?
Settings › Acknowledgements lists Box2D, the bundled fonts and the sound effects with their license text.
