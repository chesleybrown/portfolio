Scuttle: Boat Sandbox

Support · Privacy policy

Scuttle is a pixel-art water sandbox for iPhone. Pick a boat, drop it in the sea, and see what it takes to sink it.

Getting help

Found a bug, or have an idea for the sandbox? Email me@chesleybrown.ca. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

The app only works sideways.
That's intended. Scuttle is landscape only so the sea gets the full width of the screen.
It's running slowly.
Open Settings and lower the Particle budget, or set Render scale to 0.75x. Cell size 4 px is the fastest simulation.
How do I reset everything?
Settings › Restore Defaults puts every slider and toggle back to how it shipped.
Where are the third-party licenses?
Settings › Acknowledgements lists Box2D and the bundled fonts with their full license text.
